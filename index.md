<dl>
<dt>October 7, 2026</dt>
<dd>Lumen Technologies' Black Lotus Labs reports the discovery of PoeLLM, first encountered in June, a piece of malware targeting open-source AI services that uses a poem posted on GitHub as a dynamic lookup table to form a C2 server address. More than 3,400 servers have reportedly been compromised since April; LiteLLM, Ollama, Gotenberg, and Gitea were among many tools linked to an exploit-scanning and cryptocurrency-mining botnet. AI model abuse, RCE, and downstream compromise of public-facing services are also risks. Source: <a href="https://cyberscoop.com/poellm-malware-botnet-poem-lumen-black-lotus-labs/" target="_blank">CyberScoop</a></dd>
<dt>October 6, 2026</dt>
<dd>President Lee Jae Myung of South Korea announces probe into at least seven major financial institutions across the country, including Hana Bank, KB Kookmin Bank, and Shinhan Bank. Officials cite Artex AI as the suspected tool used by threat actors, and reports say at least 68,000 people were breached. Source: <a href="https://therecord.media/south-korean-bank-hacks-ai-agents" target="_blank">The Record</a></dd>
<dt>September 30, 2026</dt>
<dd>The Dutch Institute for Vulnerability Disclosure (DIVD) provides details on September 29 AI agent cyberattack, which possibly exploited a chain of two zero-day vulnerabilities in open-source ticketing system, Zammad. The flaws are identified as CVE-2026-102489 and CVE-2026-102490, and together may result in session hijacking, RCE, and privilege escalation. Source: <a href="https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/divd-says-zammad-zero-days-enabled-ai-driven-network-breach/" target="_blank">Bleeping Computer</a></dd>
<dt>September 28, 2026</dt>
<dd>Microsoft discloses June 2026 Azure cyberattack by AI-driven, agentic threat actor group, JADEPUFFER (or Storm-3168). Storage accounts, Azure Key Vault, Function Apps, and SQL databases were targeted; the threat actor took 7 minutes to get to them. The objective appeared to be destruction, rather than data exfiltration. Source: <a href="https://aviatrix.ai/threat-research-center/jadepuffer-azure-tenant-destructive-cloud-attack-2026/" target="_blank">Aviatrix</a></dd>
<dt>September 23, 2026</dt>
<dd>Gambit security researchers document a deployment of AI agents (combining Strix, Cairn, Hermes) by financially motivated attackers. The numbers: 5 days, 105 attack waves, 119+ retail sites, 600,000+ credit card records. Source: <a href="https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/malicious-ai-agents-steal-600k-credit-cards-infect-100-plus-sites-with-skimmers/" target="_blank">Bleeping Computer</a></dd>
<dt>September 22, 2026</dt>
<dd>Security researcher Patrick Wardle discovers a zero-day vulnerability in Meta's Muse AI assistant that allows adversaries to access authentication tokens via ClickFix attack. This grants full control over the agent and the resources it has permission to access (e.g., email, banking integrations). Source: <a href="https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/muse-metas-extraordinarily-privileged-ai-assistant-has-a-serious-0-day/" target="_blank">Ars Technica</a></dd>
